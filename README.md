Sankalp Laminates — Decorative Surfaces Website

A premium, responsive website designed and developed by PnP Studio for Sankalp Laminates, a decorative laminate and surface solutions business based in Vadodara, Gujarat.

The website is designed to present Sankalp Laminates as a trusted destination for architects, interior designers, modular kitchen fabricators, and homeowners looking for high-quality decorative surfaces.

✨ Features

🎨 Premium, modern UI/UX

📱 Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile

🪵 Wood Grain laminate collection

🪨 Stone & Marble finishes

✨ Gloss & Solid finishes

◼️ Matte & Textured finishes

📚 Interactive product catalogue

🧾 Sample request/enquiry system

🏭 Showroom and business information

📍 Google Maps location integration

💬 WhatsApp contact integration

⭐ Customer testimonials

🔧 Product and service highlights

📊 Admin dashboard for managing enquiries and contact details

📋 Product Range

The website showcases 500+ décor finishes, including wood, stone, marble, gloss, matte, and textured surfaces. It also highlights supporting products and services such as roof sheets, edgebands, hardware, precision cutting, and edge-banding.

🔄 Customer Journey

The website presents a simple three-step process:

Browse & Shortlist — Explore finishes online or visit the showroom.

Take Home a Sample — Request physical swatches to evaluate finishes.

Fabricate & Deliver — Confirmed sheets are cut, edge-banded, and prepared for installation.

🎯 Project Objective

The goal was to create a professional digital presence that communicates product quality, builds trust with trade professionals, simplifies catalogue discovery, and generates showroom/sample enquiries.
